Day 1 — Introduce Yourself

Goal: Speak about yourself confidently for 30–60 seconds.

Useful sentences

My name is ___.
I am from ___.
I live in ___.
I am a student / I work as a ___.
I enjoy ___.
I want to improve my English because ___.

Vocabulary

Confident — feeling comfortable speaking.

Improve — become better.

Practice — repeat something to get better.
